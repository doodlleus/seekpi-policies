Wander Beat privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Wander Beat is a rhythm game for Android by SeekingPi. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

In short: SeekingPi doesn't collect personal data from the game. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress is kept on your phone. If you're signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of it is also saved to your Google account. Google Play handles purchases.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your progress. The levels you've cleared, your stars and best scores, and the packs you own are stored in the game's private storage.
  • Your settings. Your timing calibration and game settings are stored in the game's private storage too.

Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, Wander Beat saves a copy of your progress and settings to Google Play Games' saved games. That lets your progress follow you to a new phone, or come back after you reinstall the game. The save also records the model of the phone it was made on, such as "Pixel 9". Your timing calibration isn't included, because it's specific to each phone.

Google signs you in and keeps your saved game in your Google account. Wander Beat doesn't read your Play Games name or ID, and SeekingPi doesn't receive your saved game. You can play without Google Play Games. If you do, your progress stays on your phone only. Google's privacy policy covers Google Play Games.

Purchases

Google Play handles buying a pack. SeekingPi never sees your payment details. The game is told which packs you own so it can unlock them. When it starts, it checks with Google Play so that packs you've bought come back after a reinstall.

As the seller, SeekingPi receives records of each order from Google, such as the order number, what was bought, the price and the country it was bought in. SeekingPi uses them only for refunds, tax and support. Google's privacy policy covers your purchases on Google Play.

Sharing your results

When you share a result, the game makes a picture of your score. It keeps that picture briefly in its private cache and sends it, with a short message and a link to the game on Google Play, to the app you pick. That app's privacy policy then applies.

What SeekingPi collects

Nothing from the game itself. SeekingPi doesn't receive your progress, your scores or any information about you or your phone. SeekingPi only receives the order records described above, and anything you choose to send by email.

Third parties

Wander Beat uses Google Play for purchases and Google Play Games for sign-in and saved games, as described above. It doesn't include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and SeekingPi doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone. Your progress and settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Your saved game. It stays in Google Play Games until you delete it. You can delete the game's data in your Google Play Games settings. Or email SeekingPi and we'll explain how.
  • Order records. Google and SeekingPi keep order records for as long as the law requires, for example for tax.

Children

Wander Beat is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
