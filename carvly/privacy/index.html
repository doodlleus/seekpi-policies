Carvly privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Carvly is an Android app by SeekingPi for designing carvings and running them on a CNC router from your phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: SeekingPi doesn't collect anything from the app. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reports. Your projects stay on your phone. The app talks only to the machine you choose on your own network, and files leave your phone only when you share them. If you subscribe to Carvly Pro, Google Play handles the purchase.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Your projects. Designs, the cuts planned for them, and any pictures, drawings or fonts you add to them are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Your bits and settings. Your bits, your machine's size, your choices in the app, and the addresses of machines you've connected to, so you can reconnect quickly.

Your machine and your network

Carvly connects only to the CNC machine you choose, on your local network or by USB cable. It sends the machine jobs and commands and reads back its position and status. When you search for a machine, the app listens for the machine's announcement on your WiFi and briefly tries the machine's port on addresses in your local network. Nothing goes over the internet, and nothing is sent to SeekingPi.

Photos and files

When you add a photo, from the camera or your gallery, or open a drawing, font or list of names, Carvly reads it on your phone to turn it into a design. Only the file you pick is read. It isn't uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

While a job runs, Carvly shows a notification with the job's progress, and tells you when the bit needs changing or the job ends. Android asks your permission for these, and you can turn the alerts off in the app. They're made on your phone. No notification service is involved.

Carvly Pro

Designing is free. Carving on your own machine and exporting G-code take Carvly Pro, a subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play takes your payment and keeps the record of your purchase, under Google Play's terms and Google's privacy policy. Carvly asks Google Play whether you have Carvly Pro and keeps the answer on your phone, so you can carve when the phone has no internet, such as on the machine's own WiFi. SeekingPi never sees your payment details, and Carvly doesn't send anything about your purchase anywhere. Manage or cancel Carvly Pro in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.

As the seller, SeekingPi receives records of each order from Google, such as the order number, what was bought, the price and the country it was bought in. SeekingPi uses them only for refunds, tax and support.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose to share them, such as G-code, a project, or the connection log from the Machine screen, and only to the app or person you pick. That app's privacy policy then applies. The connection log contains the commands exchanged with your machine and its local network address.

What SeekingPi collects

Nothing from the app itself. SeekingPi doesn't receive your designs, your photos or any information about you, your phone or your machine. SeekingPi only receives the order records described above, and anything you choose to send by email.

Third parties

Carvly doesn't include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and SeekingPi doesn't sell or share your data with anyone. The only other company involved is Google, which sells Carvly Pro through Google Play.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone. Everything Carvly keeps is on your phone. Delete a project in the app to remove it, or uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove everything.
  • Order records. Google and SeekingPi keep order records for as long as the law requires, for example for tax.

Children

Carvly is made for adults who use CNC machines, and isn't directed at children. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
