Hijinks privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Hijinks: Offline Party Games is an Android app by SeekingPi: a box of party games you play on your own phone or with friends' phones nearby. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: Hijinks doesn't collect or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. When you play with friends nearby, your player name and how the game is going pass directly between the phones in your game, and never to SeekingPi.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Settings and scores. Your player name, sound, music, vibration and sensitivity settings, the note you tune as your "do" for Sing, and your best times and scores are stored in the app's private storage. They may be included in your phone's own Android backup.
  • Purchases. Whether you've unlocked the games is checked with Google Play each time the app starts.

Playing with friends nearby

Multiplayer games connect phones directly to each other using Google Nearby Connections, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, with no internet or server in between. Your chosen player name and the state of the game (such as who's ready, scores, positions and who's still in) are exchanged only with the phones in your game, and only while you play. Nothing is uploaded or kept by SeekingPi.

Sensors, camera and microphone

  • Motion sensors. Many games read how your phone is moving, tilted or turned (accelerometer, gyroscope and rotation sensors). The readings are used on your phone, as you play, and never leave it.
  • Camera (Spin and Hot & Cold, optional). These games can show the live camera picture behind the game, just for fun. The picture is only drawn on your screen. It isn't recorded, stored, analysed or sent anywhere, and you can switch it off with the camera button in either game.
  • Microphone (Sing and Puff). Sing listens to work out the pitch of the note you're singing, and Puff listens to how hard you're blowing. Only the pitch or the loudness is used, live, on your phone, to move the game along. The sound itself is never recorded, stored or sent. The microphone is only on while one of these games is on screen.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, to find and connect to your friends' phones.
  • Location (Android 12L and older only). Older versions of Android require location permission before an app can look for nearby Bluetooth devices. Hijinks never reads or stores your location.
  • Camera, asked the first time Spin or Hot & Cold shows the camera, for the backdrop.
  • Microphone, asked the first time you play Sing or Puff.
  • Internet, to connect to nearby phones over Wi-Fi and for purchases through Google Play.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback.

Purchases

Hijinks is free to play, with one optional in-app purchase that unlocks every game. Google Play handles the purchase, and SeekingPi never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play which purchases you own, so an unlock you've bought comes back after a reinstall.

As the seller, SeekingPi receives records of each order from Google, such as the order number, what was bought, the price and the country it was bought in. SeekingPi uses them only for refunds, tax and support. Google's privacy policy covers your purchases on Google Play.

Third parties

Hijinks doesn't include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it doesn't share data with anyone. It uses Google Play services for nearby connections and for purchases.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings and scores stay on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google and SeekingPi keep order records for as long as the law requires, for example for tax.

Children

Hijinks isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
